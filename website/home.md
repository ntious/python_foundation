## A friendly place to begin

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **01 · Read & predict**

    Take a small example. Think about what it will do before running it.

- **02 · Run & explore**

    Open the notebook in Binder, change one value, and notice what happens.

- **03 · Test & explain**

    Compare your result with your prediction. Explain what you learned in your own words.

</div>

!!! tip "Your notebook is your practice space"
    Read lessons and copy examples here. Use each lesson’s **Run in Binder** button to write and run Python in your browser, with no account needed. Save a downloaded copy of your work before closing Binder. [See other ways to practice](setup/index.md).
